Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of personal and sensitive information by the AICheck application ("Data Controller"). This document is executed in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea and other applicable data protection frameworks.

1. Designation of Data Controller

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, the developer of the AICheck application acts as the Data Controller. The designated Chief Privacy Officer (CPO) responsible for data subject inquiries and statutory compliance can be contacted at: writtenwithred@gmail.com.

2. Scope of Data Collection

To facilitate the application's core functions, the Data Controller processes the following categories of information upon explicit user activation:

  • Account Identifiers: Email addresses and cryptographic authentication tokens.
  • Biometric and Audiovisual Data: Localized audio recordings and video frames depicting infant facial features and skin conditions.
  • Feedback and Telemetry: Textual data and system-generated metadata files submitted exclusively via the manual feedback mechanism.

3. Principle of Purpose Limitation

Data processing is strictly confined to explicitly authorized purposes. The Data Controller shall process audiovisual data solely to execute real-time inference via integrated artificial intelligence models to generate informational reports. Explicitly submitted user feedback may be reviewed offline for the sole purpose of auditing and improving the accuracy of the proprietary models. The Data Controller expressly prohibits the automated retraining of machine learning models on live user data streams.

4. Cryptographic Security Measures

The Data Controller employs a Zero-Knowledge Architecture via Client-Side Encryption. All media is encrypted on the user device utilizing AES-GCM-256 protocols via the hardware-backed Android Keystore prior to transmission. The Data Controller maintains no technical capacity to decrypt, view, or access raw media files stored on our infrastructure unless such files are explicitly decrypted and submitted by the user via the manual feedback mechanism.

5. Minor Privacy and Guardian Consent

The application processes the health and biometric data of minors under the age of fourteen (14). The Data Controller strictly requires the execution of a verifiable consent agreement by a legally authorized parent or guardian prior to data collection. Unauthorized recording of minors is strictly prohibited.

6. Data Subject Rights and Erasure

Data subjects retain the statutory right to request access, rectification, or erasure of their personal data. Upon receipt of an erasure request or the termination of an account, the Data Controller shall permanently and irreversibly destroy all associated ciphertext and cryptographic materials.
