Privacy Policy for AICheck

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide our AI-driven infant analysis, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and basic profile data provided during registration.
  • Audiovisual Data: Short audio recordings (cries) and video/image frames of the infant's face captured via your device's camera and microphone.
  • User Feedback Data: Text-based reasoning and classification corrections you manually submit regarding the AI's analysis (metadata.json).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to operate and improve the AICheck service:

  • To Provide AI Analysis: Your audio and video data are processed through our integrated AI models (including YOLOv8 for skin lesions) and the Gemini API to generate real-time infant state reports.
  • To Manually Improve Models: We do not use automated real-time retraining. Instead, the developer manually reviews your submitted feedback and associated media to filter high-quality data for future, secure, manual model updates.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Your data is securely stored and processed using third-party cloud infrastructure. We utilize:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: For secure database storage (Firestore/Realtime Database), authentication, and media storage.
  • Gemini API: Raw data metrics are sent to the Gemini API to generate natural language reports.

These third-party providers are bound by their own strict security and privacy standards.

4. Data Security

We implement robust, server-side security rules within our Firebase architecture to ensure that your audiovisual data and feedback cannot be accessed, written, or modified without verifiable cryptographic authentication and explicit consent logs.

5. Children's Privacy

Because AICheck processes the audiovisual data of minors, we require explicit, verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian before the camera or microphone can be activated. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without this mandatory guardian consent gate.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You may request the immediate deletion of your account, your child's media files, and your submitted feedback at any time by utilizing the deletion function within the App's profile settings or by contacting the developer directly.
