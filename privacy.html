Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") governs the collection, use, and processing of personal and sensitive information by the AICheck application ("Data Controller," "we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with applicable data protection frameworks, including the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea.

1. Scope of Data Collection

In order to provide the core functionality of the AICheck application, we process the following categories of data:

  • Account Data: Identifiers including email address, username, and cryptographic authentication tokens.
  • Audiovisual & Biometric Data: Localized audio recordings and video frames of an infant's face and skin, captured strictly upon explicit user activation.
  • Telemetry & Feedback: Manual submissions comprising text-based corrections and associated `metadata.json` files generated during the evaluation of the AI models.

2. Purpose Limitation and Processing

We strictly adhere to the principle of purpose limitation. Data is processed exclusively for the following authorized purposes:

  • Executing real-time inference via integrated computer vision models (e.g., YOLOv8) and the Gemini API to generate informational state reports.
  • Facilitating the manual, offline review of explicitly submitted user feedback to improve the accuracy and efficacy of the underlying artificial intelligence models.

We expressly prohibit the automated retraining of models on live user data streams.

3. Cryptographic Security & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

We implement a Zero-Knowledge Architecture utilizing Client-Side Encryption. All audiovisual media is encrypted on the user's device utilizing AES-256-GCM algorithms via the hardware-backed Android Keystore prior to cloud transmission. Consequently, raw media files stored in our infrastructure remain cryptographically inaccessible to the Data Controller unless explicitly decrypted by the originating user device or explicitly authorized via the manual feedback mechanism.

4. Guardian Consent & Children's Privacy

As the application processes the sensitive data of minors, utilization of the camera and microphone is strictly embargoed pending the execution of a verifiable Clickwrap agreement by a legally authorized parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process data from individuals under the age of 14 without such statutory consent.

5. Data Subject Rights & Erasure

Data subjects retain comprehensive rights regarding their personal information. You possess the right to request access, rectification, portability, and erasure of your data at any time. Upon account termination or explicit request, all associated cryptographic keys, feedback payloads, and ciphertext stored within our infrastructure shall be permanently and irreversibly destroyed.
